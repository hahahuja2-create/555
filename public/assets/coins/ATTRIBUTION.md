# Asset logos

These files identify the corresponding crypto market symbols. They were downloaded from the Trust Wallet asset registry and CoinGecko image metadata; each image's original source URL is recorded in `manifest.json`.

- Registry: https://github.com/trustwallet/assets
- Metadata: https://docs.coingecko.com/reference/coins-markets

Coin logos remain the marks of their respective owners. TaskMall's hexagonal T mark in `../brand/` is an original, separate design.

The market feed is supplied separately. CoinGecko metadata is not used to price TaskMall positions.
